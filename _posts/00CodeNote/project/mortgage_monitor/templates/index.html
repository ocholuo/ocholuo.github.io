Data Sources
Mortgage News Daily — Real lender rate sheets, updated ~4PM ET weekdays. Most timely source for daily rate movement.
Bankrate / Freddie Mac PMMS — Freddie Mac updates weekly (Thu); Bankrate daily. Used for trend confirmation and 15yr/ARM data.
FRED (St. Louis Fed) — Historical weekly series MORTGAGE30US, MORTGAGE15US, MORTGAGE5US used for long-term charts and percentile calculations.
Rates shown are national averages for informational purposes only. Individual rates vary based on credit score, LTV, and lender. Not financial advice.
