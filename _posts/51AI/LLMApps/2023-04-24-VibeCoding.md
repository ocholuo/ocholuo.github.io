## Case Challenge

- Producing a bare prototype is no longer enough but leverage AI for high-quality, efficient output.
- invest more time in deep thinking and divergent brainstorming; let AI own the 0-to-1 document generation.

**deep execution power**:

- Deconstructs complex tasks into manageable sub-tasks
- Organizes information systematically
- Converts research into a polished, professional end-product

Steps:

1. Build a Research Framework via Multi-turn Dialogue
   1. Goal: extract a structured mental model of the problem space before writing deliverable.
   2. **Initial prompt**: Start broad — e.g., *"Create a product plan for Enterprise Knowledge Management"*
   3. **Iterate**: Ask follow-ups to surface core pain points, existing solutions, and how AI is reshaping the space
   4. **Deep dive**: Use AI to compare specific competitors (e.g., Notion vs. Confluence) across positioning, AI capabilities, and pricing

2. Design the Logic for Specific Cases
   1. Extract core insights from Step 1, then craft targeted prompts for each business scenario:
   2. **ToB Product Solutions**: Existing pain points + lessons from current market leaders
   3. **ToC Product Opportunities**: Market trends, user behavior shifts, and stable human needs
   4. **Competitive Differentiation**: Functions, use-case scenarios, and pricing to find a unique "hook"

3. Generate Research Reports
   1. convert research logic into a full document:
   2. AI decomposes complex research topics into granular sub-tasks
   3. Executes web searches per sub-task
   4. Organizes and synthesizes findings into a structured report
   5. **Output:** A complete, detailed report with a traceable index of information sources.

4. One-Click Presentation Generation
   1. Feed the generated research report into the AI's PPT/PDF/... creation tool
   2. Upload a reference template for style/layout guidance
   3. Spend ~10 minutes polishing: refine copy, swap layouts as needed
   4. Export directly to PDF or PPT — no need to open Word or PowerPoint
